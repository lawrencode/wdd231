NP Membership Benefits

Community-focused membership at no cost for qualifying nonprofit organizations.

  • Free Chamber directory listing
  • Invitations to selected nonprofit networking events
  • Access to community partnership and volunteer opportunities

Bronze Membership Benefits

Annual investment: $300. Build local connections and establish your business presence.

  • Business directory listing
  • Access to monthly networking mixers
  • Member pricing for Chamber events

Silver Membership Benefits

Annual investment: $600. Grow your visibility and strengthen your team's business skills.

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Featured directory placement and one business spotlight
  • Discounted training and event sponsorship opportunities

Gold Membership Benefits

Annual investment: $1,200. Make a larger impact with premium promotion and leadership access.

  • All Silver benefits
  • Premium homepage spotlight and priority advertising
  • Complimentary training seats and priority event discounts
  • Invitation to executive roundtables and leadership events
