Website project plan

Nampa Valley Chamber

A planning document for a welcoming online hub connecting Nampa-area businesses and community members.

Site Name

Nampa Valley Chamber

The name identifies the organization and the community it serves. “Nampa Valley” gives the chamber a clear local identity, while “Chamber” signals its role in supporting and connecting businesses.

Optional domain: nampavalleychamber.org

Site Purpose

The website helps businesses and residents discover local organizations, chamber membership opportunities, community events, and business resources. It will introduce the chamber, highlight member businesses, share timely local information, and make it easy to contact the organization or learn how to join.

Audience Scenarios

These questions represent common needs of prospective members, local business owners, and people looking to connect with the Nampa community.

  • How can I find local businesses that are members of the Nampa Valley Chamber?
  • What events are coming up, and where and when are they being held?
  • What are the benefits of joining the chamber, and how do I apply?
  • How can I contact the chamber about a business question or partnership?

Color Scheme

The plan uses a warm, community-oriented palette. Navy communicates trust and professionalism; warm orange adds energy and draws attention to calls to action. Light backgrounds keep the content readable and open.

  • Nampa navy — #143D59
    Navigation, section headings, and primary links.

  • Deep navy — #0D2D43
    Hero backgrounds and high-emphasis text.

  • Warm orange — #D9773D
    Accent labels, highlights, and calls to action.

  • Warm ivory — #F7F1EA
    Page background and soft contrast areas.

Typography

Playfair Display
Used for page titles and major section headings to add a distinctive, welcoming editorial feel. Example: Build stronger local connections.
Open Sans
Used for body copy, navigation, labels, and buttons because it is clear and readable across screen sizes. Example: Discover events, resources, and businesses in the Nampa Valley.

Home Page Wireframe

The home page layout is planned for both a narrow mobile viewport and a larger desktop/tablet viewport. The structure includes a header with brand and navigation, a hero introduction and join button, event and weather information, member spotlights, and a contact footer.
