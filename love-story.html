How it began

A dream come true

Some love stories begin with a spark, but ours began with a quiet crush. I liked him since middle school, but he never seemed to notice me. While he was simply living his life, I was admiring him from afar and secretly hoping that one day he would see me the way I saw him. Years passed, and life took us on different paths. I never imagined that the person I had liked for so long would eventually become such an important part of my life. But sometimes, life has a way of bringing two people together when the time is right. Now we're together, and it feels like fate. What once seemed like an impossible dream has become my reality. Every moment we share reminds me how grateful I am that our story didn't end before it even began. Looking back, I realize that some things are worth waiting for. The boy I once admired from a distance is now the person who makes me smile every day. Our journey is proof that dreams really can come true, and that sometimes the best love stories are the ones that take time to unfold. "I liked you for years, and now I get to love you forever." ❤️

Love is not just the big romantic moments; it is the little things—your voice, your patience, the way you listen, the way you make me feel seen. That is what made me fall in love with you, and that is what keeps my heart choosing you every single day.
