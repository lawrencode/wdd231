How it began

A dream come true

It’s sweet when a crush turns into something real, especially after so much time has passed. There was a time when you were just a boy I admired from afar. Back in school, I had the biggest crush on you, but you never seemed to notice me. While I dreamed about what it would be like to talk to you, laugh with you, and spend time with you, life kept moving forward. Eventually, I convinced myself to move on, thinking that maybe we were never meant to be. But sometimes life has its own plans, and what is meant to be has a way of finding its path. Years later, fate brought us back together in a way I never expected. The boy I once admired from a distance became the person I get to love every day. Looking back now, it's amazing to think that someone who was once just a silent crush is now my best friend, my safe place, and the person I imagine my future with. What felt impossible back then has become the most beautiful reality. Since we've been together, we've created so many memories that I will treasure forever. From our simple dates filled with laughter to the quiet moments where we could talk for hours, every moment has brought us closer. Some of my favorite memories are the times we spent together doing the simplest things, because no matter where we were or what we were doing, being together always made it special. We would talk about our dreams, our goals, and the future we hope to build together. Those conversations made me realize that our love wasn't just about the present, but about creating a life side by side.

Love is not just the big romantic moments; it is the little things—your voice, your patience, the way you listen, the way you make me feel seen. That is what made me fall in love with you, and that is what keeps my heart choosing you every single day. What makes our story so special is that it reminds me that timing is everything. I liked you from afar when we were younger, never imagining that one day I would be holding your hand and planning a future with you. Now, instead of wondering what could have been, I get to live the love story I once only dreamed about. You are proof that some people come into our lives exactly when they're meant to, and that sometimes the best love stories begin long before they actually start. I used to be the girl who quietly admired you from a distance. Now, I am the girl who gets to love you openly, deeply, and forever. And if our journey has taught me anything, it's that fate has a beautiful way of bringing two hearts together when the time is right. ❤️
